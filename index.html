Booking Guidelines

Transparent Terms & Billing Policy

Directly outlined from our official brochure (Page 11) to ensure clear understanding and zero surprises.

Payment & Confirmation

  • 50% Advance required on the day of confirmation/booking to lock your date.
  • 30% Payment to be cleared 15 days before the event date.
  • Remaining 20% Balance payable immediately upon conclusion of the event.
  • Advance payments are non-refundable under any circumstances due to reserved scheduling.

Venue & Utilities Arrangements

  • Function hall utensils, crockery, cutlery, furniture & labor payments borne by the booking party (or included as agreed).
  • Water supply for cooking, cleaning & drinking, plus adequate electricity at cooking and dining zones to be provided.
  • Landscaping, zoomers, and special lighting arrangements at dining area to be coordinated with the venue.

Extra Plates & Logistics

  • Any extra plates consumed beyond the booked count will be billed at the full agreed plate rate.
  • Additional live counters or specialized setup requests will be charged additionally.
  • Transportation charges applicable depending upon venue distance outside standard Secunderabad limits.
  • Sales and service taxes (GST) applicable as per government regulations.
